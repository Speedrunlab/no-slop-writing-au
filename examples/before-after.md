# Before and After

## Before (AI slop)

As we navigate the complexities of our Q3 performance in an ever-changing market landscape, it's important to delve into the key metrics and unpack what they signal for our strategy going forward. This underscores the need for a revised approach to customer acquisition.

Moreover, by leveraging our learnings from the previous quarter, we can unlock synergies across teams and take a more holistic approach to growth. Additionally, this is a powerful opportunity to lean into our strengths and foster a culture of accountability.

Key priorities for Q4:

- **Alignment:** Ensure all stakeholders are on the same page regarding targets.
- **Execution:** Focus on actionable next steps to drive meaningful impact.
- **Clarity:** Streamline our communication to fundamentally reshape how we engage customers.

Ultimately, the goal is to build a more resilient and agile organisation that can thrive in any environment.

## After (human-written)

Q3 revenue was $4.2M, down 8% from Q2. The drop came from two places: enterprise deal cycles stretched from 45 to 68 days, and our mid-market churn hit 6.1%, the highest since January.

Two changes for Q4. First, we are splitting the enterprise sales team into hunters and farmers. Hunters close new logos. Farmers own renewals and expansion. This should cut the cycle time back down because reps stop juggling both. Second, we are adding a 30-day check-in call for every mid-market account. The churn data shows most cancellations happen between day 45 and day 60, which means we are losing people before they see value.

Target for Q4: $4.8M revenue, sub-4% mid-market churn, enterprise cycle back under 50 days.

## Anti-Patterns Present in the "Before" Version

1. Pattern 3: navigate the complexities, ever-changing landscape
2. Pattern 1: delve into, unpack
3. Pattern 2: this underscores
4. Pattern 4: leveraging our learnings, synergies, holistic approach
5. Pattern 6: Moreover and Additionally as paragraph openers
6. Pattern 8: corporate therapist voice ("powerful opportunity to lean into our strengths")
7. Pattern 7: bold-colon-explanation bullets (Alignment, Execution, Clarity)
8. Pattern 13: fundamentally reshape
9. Pattern 9: neat bow conclusion ("Ultimately, the goal is to build a more resilient...")
10. Pattern 10: first two paragraphs say nothing specific

The "After" version contains the same type of information (Q3 results, Q4 plan) but with specific numbers, named causes, concrete actions, and a measurable target. Every sentence says something you can act on.
